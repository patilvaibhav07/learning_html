About Us

Once upon a time, a small red fox named Rusty lived in a green forest. Rusty found a big, sweet red apple under a tree. He wanted to eat it all by himself.Just then, a little brown rabbit hopped by. The rabbit looked very tired and hungry. Rusty looked at the apple, and then he looked at the rabbit. Rusty remembered what his mother taught him: sharing brings joy.

So, Rusty decided to share the apple with the rabbit. He broke the apple into two pieces and gave one piece to the rabbit. The rabbit's eyes lit up with happiness, and he thanked Rusty for his kindness. They both enjoyed the delicious apple together, and from that day on, Rusty and the rabbit became the best of friends.
